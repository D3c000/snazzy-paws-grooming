Dog grooming tips

🪮

Brush little and often

Brush your dog's coat gently several times a week, using a tool suited to their coat type. Regular brushing helps reduce tangles.

🐾

Check paws and nails

Watch for nails clicking on floors or discomfort when walking. Ask a groomer or vet if you are unsure how short to trim.

🛁

Use dog-safe products

Use shampoos designed for dogs, rinse thoroughly and dry carefully. For skin irritation or persistent itching, consult a veterinarian.

Our story

Friendly local grooming

Snazzy Paws Grooming was started by two dog lovers who wanted to offer professional and caring grooming in Hamilton. Today, the owners work with two employees to help dogs look and feel their best.

We welcome local families and visitors from surrounding towns. Ask our team about a grooming option that suits your dog's age, coat and needs.
